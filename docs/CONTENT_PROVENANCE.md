### Public statistics shown

**SatQuery: measured 28 September 2026.** The experimental paired RGB/19-answer specialist achieved 69.90% exact overall answer accuracy on official CDVQA Test-1: 39,686 questions over 968 pairs. Question-only control: 68.06%. Official Train/Val were used for training/selection; the report specifies encoder, seed, controls and prediction-based recomputation. Twelve test pairs had prior inspection; exclusion analysis is disclosed. Test-2 uses the same imagery and overlapping questions and must not be pooled with Test-1. The profile omits Test-2 to avoid a second headline and does not call this overall SatQuery accuracy. [Full methods and tables](https://github.com/Yoriichi-GOD/SATquery-AI/blob/main/docs/CDVQA_FINAL_REPORT.md).

**REFLEX: evidence indexed by the 25 October 2026 audit.** The underlying saved image study uses 860 distinct VisA photographs: 350 previously exposed replay cases, 255 new-category development cases, 255 final cases. These partitions sum to 860; method rows/transforms are not additional independent images. The primary candidate was fixed before final evaluation. Equal-category macro image AUROC on the 255-case final population declined from 0.852044 to 0.840978 (profile rounds to 0.8520 → 0.8410). Threshold decisions and ranking are different outcomes. The 25 October date belongs to the evidence audit/index, not a new model run; an exact single measurement timestamp for the whole study is not asserted. [Dated audit](https://github.com/Yoriichi-GOD/REFLEX/blob/main/benchmarks/intelligence_20261025/README.md), [study populations](https://github.com/Yoriichi-GOD/REFLEX/blob/main/benchmarks/anomaly_v2/README.md), [release assessment](https://github.com/Yoriichi-GOD/REFLEX/blob/main/benchmarks/anomaly_v2/RELEASE_ASSESSMENT.md).

**GhostTrace: generated 25 October 2026, 11:33:59 UTC.** Public sample replay compared `sample_deletion_requests.csv` with participant-facing `sample_output.csv`. Status agreement is 29/32; exact ordered step sequences are 8/32. These are the same 32 development requests. They are not independent populations, hidden-label performance, real-world deletion verification or legal certification. The reference also has policy disputes disclosed in the report. [Evaluation and limitations](https://github.com/Yoriichi-GOD/GhostTrace/blob/main/code/evaluation/evaluation_report.md).
